# Delete your Finnon account

**Last updated:** February 15, 2026

Finnon allows you to delete your account and all associated data directly from the application. When you delete your account, all your personal data will be permanently removed, including your email address, financial transactions, accounts, goals, and any other information you have entered.

---

## How to delete your account

1. Open the Finnon app on your device.
2. Go to Settings > Account > General.
3. In the "Danger zone" section, select "Delete account".
4. Confirm the deletion.

---

## What happens when you delete your account

Deletion is permanent and irreversible. All your data will be deleted within a maximum of 30 days.

If you share financial accounts with other users, your access will be revoked but the shared account data will not be affected for other users.

---

## Can't access the app?

If you cannot access the app and need to delete your account, send an email to **contacto@finnon.app** from the email address associated with your account.
